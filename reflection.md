# Reflection

This project taught me how to use AI to help me build a project from scratch. I didn’t just give it one huge prompt, I basically had a conversation with the AI and worked through everything step by step. I asked it to help me create the SQLite database, write the Python code, make the API, and test everything.

I also realised that AI isn’t always right. Sometimes it made mistakes in the code, misunderstood what I wanted, or gave me solutions that were way more complicated than they needed to be. I

One of the main things I learnt was that better prompts = better results. Giving the AI more context and being specific about what I wanted made a big difference. I also learnt that prompting is more of an ongoing conversation rather than just asking one question and getting a perfect answer.

Overall, this project showed me that AI can make coding much easier, but I still need to understand and check what it produces instead of blindly copying it.
